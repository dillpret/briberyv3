If you're having trouble thinking of a prompt, there is a button to enter a random one!
Players can join after the game starts and will be able to take part as soon as the next round starts - Just make sure they're online with their devices open when the round is beginning!
You can leave and rejoin if you need to, your score will remain and you can pick up again with the next round
Players can see who a prompt comes from, so the better you know a person the better you can come up with a bribe to win them over
You get the most points for having the winning bribe, but bonus coins from other players can give you an edge to break a tie
